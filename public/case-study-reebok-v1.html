Abstract 3D material exploration for Reebok's sustainability campaign — bouncy simulations, tactile surfaces, and buttery smooth animation. Every element designed to make recycled material feel premium and desirable.

I handled 3D modeling, simulation, and animation — building out a system of material behaviors that felt cohesive across multiple deliverables and aspect ratios. The focus was on tactility: making things you want to touch.
